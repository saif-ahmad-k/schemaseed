How Pro works

This short recording shows a Pro session on the sample store: three tables, 200 rows, foreign keys, chaos, Japanese names, and Oracle.

What the clip shows

  • Pro is on, so every table is generated, up to 10,000 rows.
  • Orders use user and product ids from the rows just created.
  • Chaos leaves a few cells blank or messy. Primary keys stay valid.
  • Names and cities can be Japanese, Chinese, Russian, or Arabic.
  • The SQL file follows the database you pick, including Oracle.

Free is the first table and 50 rows. Pro is $2 for 24 hours or $7 for a month.
